# Lateral reading and verification

## Lateral reading

Instead of staying on a page and judging it by how professional it looks
("vertical reading"), open new tabs and find out what other independent
sources say about the site and the claim. This is how professional
fact-checkers work and it is faster and more accurate than scrutinising
the page in isolation.

## The core checks (SIFT)

1. **Stop.** Notice your emotional reaction; do not share yet.
2. **Investigate the source.** Who runs this outlet? What is their track
   record and funding? A one-line search often settles it.
3. **Find better coverage.** Look for the same claim reported by outlets
   with editorial standards. If only fringe sites carry it, that is
   informative.
4. **Trace claims, quotes, and media to the original context.** Follow the
   link, find the study, watch the full video.

## Quick signals

- A named author with a history of real bylines.
- A visible corrections policy and dated updates.
- An "About" page with a real organisation and address.
- Outbound links to primary sources rather than only to itself.
