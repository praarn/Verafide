# Financial, crypto, and scam framing

## Recurring hooks

- **Guaranteed returns / no risk:** any promise of guaranteed profit is a
  red flag; real investments carry risk.
- **Urgency and scarcity:** "closes tonight", "only 10 spots", "price
  doubles at midnight" to prevent due diligence.
- **Fake authority:** forged endorsements from public figures, cloned news
  sites, and fabricated "as seen on" logos.
- **Insider secret:** a method banks/governments supposedly want hidden.
- **Screenshot proof:** unverifiable account balances and testimonials.
- **Affiliate incentive:** the loudest promoters are paid per referral.

## Cloned-newsroom scams

Scam operations copy the layout, fonts, and logo of a real outlet to host
a fake interview or endorsement. Check the domain carefully (look-alike
spellings, odd TLDs), and see whether the story exists on the real
outlet's own site.

## Checking

Verify registration and licensing with the relevant financial regulator.
Search the project or promoter name with "scam" and "review". Confirm any
celebrity endorsement on that person's own verified channels — it almost
never exists.
