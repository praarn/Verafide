# Propaganda and state-media techniques

## Common devices

- **Whataboutism:** deflecting a criticism by pointing to an unrelated
  fault of the accuser instead of addressing the claim.
- **Firehose of falsehood:** high-volume, rapid, repetitive messaging
  across many channels; contradictions are not a problem because the goal
  is to exhaust and confuse, not to persuade of one story.
- **Manufactured experts:** think-tanks, "independent journalists", and
  commentators funded or coordinated by a state, presented as neutral.
- **Selective framing:** true facts arranged to imply a false conclusion;
  omission rather than fabrication.
- **Flag-planting and false attribution:** staged events or
  misattributed atrocities.

## Identifying state-aligned outlets

Check ownership and funding. Outlets such as RT, Sputnik, Press TV, Global
Times, CGTN, and Xinhua operate under government editorial control; they
can be accurate on uncontroversial topics and unreliable where the state
has an interest. Look for consistent alignment with one government's
positions across every contested story.

## Reader response

Identify the specific factual claim, check it against independent
reporting from outlets with different national and political vantage
points, and note what the piece leaves out.
