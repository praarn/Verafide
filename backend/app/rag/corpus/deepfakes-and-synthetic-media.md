# Deepfakes and synthetic media

## Video and audio tells

- Lip movements slightly out of sync with speech; teeth or tongue that
  blur or lack detail.
- Unnatural blink rate, or eyes that do not track consistently.
- A face that stays sharp while the neck, ears, or hairline flicker.
- Lighting on the face that does not match the scene.
- Voice clones: flat prosody, missing breaths, odd pacing, artifacts on
  plosive sounds, and background noise that cuts in and out.

## Context tells (often more reliable than pixels)

- No independent outlet has the same footage.
- The clip first appears on an anonymous or brand-new account.
- The statement is unusually convenient for one side and out of character
  for the speaker.
- No time, place, or event is specified.

## Verification

Look for the original, full-length source. Check whether reputable
newsrooms or the named person's official channels reference the event.
Provenance signals (C2PA / Content Credentials) are emerging but not yet
universal. When a clip cannot be traced to a verifiable origin, treat it
as unconfirmed.
