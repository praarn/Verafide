# Coordinated inauthentic behavior and astroturfing

## What it is

A campaign engineered to look like organic grassroots sentiment: many
accounts, centrally directed, amplifying the same message to fake
consensus or drown out other views.

## Signals

- Clusters of accounts created around the same date with sparse, generic
  profiles.
- Identical or near-identical phrasing posted in a short window.
- Reply spam that is off-topic but on-message.
- Sudden hashtag spikes driven by low-follower accounts.
- Cross-platform copy-paste of the same "personal" testimony.
- Engagement that is all shares/retweets with little genuine conversation.

## Why it matters for credibility

"Everyone is saying this" is a persuasion tactic. Trending status and high
engagement are not evidence a claim is true; both are easy to manufacture.

## Checking

Look at *who* is amplifying a claim, not just how much. Sample the
accounts. Check whether any established reporter or institution has
independently verified the underlying facts, separate from the volume of
posts about it.
